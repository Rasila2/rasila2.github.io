The moon spills silver across the dark sea, soft as breath.
Each wave catches it, holds it, then lets it slip away in tiny sparks.
Above, stars blink like shy friends watching from the sky.
My brush tries to keep that quiet glow—the hush between water and light—
so you can stand there with me, barefoot on wet sand, feeling the night breathe.
