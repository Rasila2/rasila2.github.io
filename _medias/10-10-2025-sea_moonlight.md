The moon spills silver across the dark sea, soft as breath. <br>
Each wave catches it, holds it, then lets it slip away in tiny sparks.<br>
Above, stars blink like shy friends watching from the sky.<br>
My brush tries to keep that quiet glow—the hush between water and light—<br>
so you can stand there with me, barefoot on wet sand, feeling the night breathe.<br>
